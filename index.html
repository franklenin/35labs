01 — Manifesto

Every image that ever existed lives hidden in mathematical space, waiting to be dreamed into form.

35 Labs is an AI-native film studio based in Amsterdam. We make animated short films using generative models — not as a gimmick, but as a genuine medium with its own aesthetic language.

We believe the frontier between human imagination and machine synthesis is where the most interesting cinema is being made right now. We are building there, frame by frame.

Our work is directed by humans who understand both the emotional logic of storytelling and the mathematical logic of diffusion models. The result is something neither could make alone.

We make cinema for the Netherlands and Mexico — two cultures with a deep appetite for visual storytelling and an openness to the genuinely new.

02 — Our Approach

01 / Write First

Story Drives
the Machine.

Every film begins with a script and a storyboard. The story drives the machine — not the other way around. We are storytellers who happen to use AI as our camera.

02 / Consistency

Consistent
Worlds, Souls.

Character consistency across AI-generated shots is a hard technical problem. We solve it through fine-tuned character models and a rigorous production pipeline built in-house.

03 / Emotion

Made to
Feel Something.

The measure of every frame is not technical quality but emotional truth. If it doesn't move you, it doesn't ship. That standard guides everything — from the prompt to the color grade.

Founder & Technical Director

His journey into AI began in philosophy — specifically with Masanao Toda's thought experiment about autonomous agents. That question pulled him through cognitive neuroscience, computer vision, and into generative AI. Now he applies the same question to cinema: how do you build something synthetic that genuinely feels.

Creative Producer

Her intellectual home is the edge of language — she wrote her thesis on Wittgenstein, the philosopher who argued that what cannot be said must be shown. Cinema is the logical conclusion of that insight. She built El Olvidado from an idea into a place people chose as their own.
